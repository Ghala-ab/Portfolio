Featured Projects

Capstone Project

A Smart Non-Invasive System for Detecting Elevated Intracranial Pressure (ICP)

3rd Place - Taif University Graduation Projects Competition Patent Application - Under Formal Examination SAIP

The Challenge

The need for a safe, reliable, and non-invasive approach to continuous ICP monitoring.

The Solution

We developed a smart, non-invasive system that measures the tympanic membrane’s response to acoustic and pressure stimuli, aiming to extract signals that can be analyzed to support the detection of elevated ICP.

My Role

Team Lead, responsible for hardware integration and signal processing, while coordinating the team’s technical work.

Prototype & Outcome

A working prototype was developed and practically tested using a simulated ear model. The project successfully reached the prototype stage, secured 3rd place in the Graduation Projects Competition, and an official patent application was filed.
